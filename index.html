StorageWise Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 26, 2026

StorageWise (“the App”) is a storage unit conversion and calculation application developed by DEXTRA HUB LIMITED (“we,” “us,” or “our”).

We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your information. This Privacy Policy explains how StorageWise handles information when you use the App.

1. Information We Collect

StorageWise is designed to operate primarily offline.

We do not collect, sell, or share personal information through the core functionality of the App.

The App does not require you to create an account or provide your name, email address, phone number, physical location, contacts, photos, videos, or other personal information.

StorageWise does not collect:

  • Name or other personal identifiers
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Contacts
  • Precise or approximate location
  • Photos or videos
  • Camera or microphone recordings
  • Health information
  • Financial or payment information
  • Authentication information
  • SMS or call information

2. Information Stored on Your Device

StorageWise may store certain app preferences and locally generated information on your device, such as:

  • App settings and preferences
  • Favorite conversions
  • Recent conversion history

This information is used solely to provide the App’s functionality.

The information is stored locally on your device and is not transmitted to our servers by StorageWise.

3. How We Use Information

Information stored locally by StorageWise may be used to:

  • Remember your app preferences
  • Display your saved favorite conversions
  • Display your recent conversions
  • Improve your experience when using the App

We do not use this locally stored information for advertising or user profiling.

4. Data Sharing

StorageWise does not sell, rent, or share personal information with third parties.

The App’s core functionality does not require personal information to be transmitted to us or to third parties.

5. Internet Access

StorageWise is designed to provide its core storage conversion and calculation functionality without requiring an internet connection.

If the App’s functionality changes in a way that affects how information is collected, processed, or transmitted, this Privacy Policy will be updated accordingly.

6. Data Security

StorageWise does not maintain a central account database containing users’ personal information.

Information stored locally on your device is subject to the security protections provided by your device and operating system.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

Locally stored preferences, favorites, and conversion history remain on your device until you remove them through the App, clear the App’s data, or uninstall the App.

You can delete locally stored information by using the relevant options within StorageWise or by clearing the App’s data through your Android device settings.

Because StorageWise does not require an account, there is no StorageWise account containing personal information that requires account deletion.

8. Children’s Privacy

StorageWise is not specifically directed toward children under the age of 13.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Third-Party Services

The current core functionality of StorageWise does not require third-party services that collect personal information.

If third-party SDKs or services are introduced in future versions and they collect or process information, this Privacy Policy will be updated to accurately describe those practices.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to StorageWise, applicable laws, or our privacy practices.

When changes are made, we will update the “Last updated” date at the top of this policy.

We encourage users to review this Privacy Policy periodically.
